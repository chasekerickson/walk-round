Walk round a set

Open a walk round page you were sent and walk through the set with its real lights, talent and furniture.

  1. Save the file you were sent to Files (in Messages or Mail: press and hold it, then Save to Files; a download from a link lands in Downloads).
  2. Tap Choose the walk round file above and pick it. Its name ends in “walk round.html”.
  3. Left thumb walks, right thumb looks round. The buttons at the top change floors or let you fly.
The file is opened on this phone only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
